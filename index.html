What waiting and loans cost

Guess what your monthly saving becomes, then see the real number. New: enter a loan the way it was offered to you and see what a year of it really costs.

Ứng dụng cho iPhone · sắp có trên App Store

Guess first

Set a monthly amount, a rate and a number of years, and commit to what you think it becomes. Then see the real figure, the gap, and a chart of interest taken out against interest left in.

What waiting costs

Start one, three or five years later and see what waiting hands back and what it takes away.

The real price of a loan

Enter a loan the way it was offered to you — equal instalments, a flat rate, a daily collection, so much per million per day, buying on instalments, borrow once and repay once — and see the real yearly rate, the total cost and the schedule.

The Seed Book

Every scenario, loan and goal you looked at, kept on your phone, by week, month, quarter or year. Tap one to open it again.

No account, no network

Everything stays on your iPhone. A calculation, not financial or legal advice.
