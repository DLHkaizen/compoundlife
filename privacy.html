Privacy Policy

Compound Life collects no data. No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no networking code.

  • The numbers you enter, your Seed Book and your settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Four counters (app opened, guess made, cost of waiting reached, picture saved) are kept on your iPhone only. They appear in Export my data and are erased by Reset the app.
  • Anything leaves your iPhone only when you tap Share or Export, to the place you choose.
  • Photos access is add-only and asked for only when you save the cost-of-waiting picture. No camera, no location, no contacts.
  • Deleting the app, or Reset the app, removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else.
