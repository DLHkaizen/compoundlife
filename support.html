Hỏi đáp

Does Compound Life lend money?

No. It is a calculator: you type the terms of a loan you were offered and it shows what a year of it really costs. It offers no loan, names no lender and links to none.

Why is every amount in Vietnamese đồng?

The app carries no exchange rate and never converts. What it shows are ratios, and those are the same in any currency.

Where is my Seed Book kept?

On your iPhone only. Settings lets you export it as plain text or erase it.

Does it need the internet?

No. It works offline and sends nothing anywhere.
